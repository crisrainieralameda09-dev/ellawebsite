Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LET Reviewer PH

Is the app really free?

Yes! LET Reviewer PH is 100% free. The app comes with a pre-configured Gemini API key, so you can start using it immediately without any costs.

Do I need to register or login?

No registration required! Just download, install, and start studying. Your chat history is saved locally on your device.

Does it work offline?

The app requires internet to communicate with the AI tutor. However, your chat history is saved locally and can be viewed offline.

How accurate is the AI tutor?

The AI is powered by Google's Gemini model and trained on comprehensive LET materials. It provides accurate, helpful responses but should be used alongside official study materials.

Can I use my own API key?

Yes! While the app comes with a pre-configured key, you can update it anytime via the Settings screen if you want to use your own Gemini API key.

Is my data safe and private?

Absolutely. All data is stored locally on your device. Your questions are sent to Google's Gemini API for responses, but no personal information is collected or shared.
